# Rights, exclusions and test accounts

This is a source-available research artifact. No blanket open-source license is asserted or granted for the entire package; code/paper copyright holders retain their rights, pending an explicit project license decision. Do not relabel it as MIT/Apache licensed.

IEEEtran.cls and IEEEtran.bst retain their original headers and licensing terms. Photograph credits/conditions are retained in figures/assets/PHOTO_CREDIT.md and SOURCES.json: the ESP32 image is CC0; the laptop and mini-PC images use the Pexels License. Photographs are illustrative, not evidence of a multi-device testbed or endorsement.

P-Verifier and CloudFix raw repositories/policies and derived task files embedding those policies are excluded because blanket redistribution permission is unresolved. Source commits, acquisition/audit explanation and numeric summaries remain. See docs/DATA_AUDIT.md and PUBLIC_TASK_REVIEW.md. Public AWS counts are inspectable but their original-policy repair experiment is not reproduced by this standalone package; obtain original sources lawfully and separately.

Mosquitto binaries/plugins, draw.io Desktop and third-party Python distributions are not bundled. Obtain the fixed versions from their official sources and follow their licenses. Synthetic local accounts (reader/publisher/observer) and the documented `spr-local-test-only` password are test fixtures only, not production secrets. No GitHub token, real credential or private key is included.

Public log/configuration copies replace local workspace/user paths with <PROJECT>/<USER>. Numerical values, decisions and message IDs are not edited. SOURCE_HASHES.json records both original and published hashes. Reported byte measurements refer to the original measured configurations, not a redacted broker .conf file.
